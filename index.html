What DinoStream offers

An overview of DinoStream's core areas.

📱 Screen Streaming

Stream your Android device's gameplay or screen. DinoStream focuses on making the streaming setup process simple and accessible.

▶️ YouTube Connection

Users can choose to connect a YouTube account to enable streaming-related features. This connection is optional and explicit — DinoStream does not access YouTube data without the user's consent.

🦴 DinoReward

DinoReward is the rewards and account system associated with DinoStream. Earn Dino Points and redeem them toward Premium. See the DinoReward page for details.

⭐ Premium

Go ad-free and unlock multiple text and image overlays with DinoStream Premium. See the Premium page for pricing and benefits.

DinoStream uses Google Sign-In for authentication. DinoStream never asks for your Google password directly — sign-in is handled entirely by Google's own secure authentication flow.
