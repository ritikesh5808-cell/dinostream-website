DinoStream Account Deletion

Last updated: August 2026

DinoStream users can request deletion of their account and associated data at any time.

How to request account deletion

To request account deletion, please contact us at:

Email: support@dinoapps.in

Include the Google account email address used with DinoStream in your request.

What data will be deleted

When an account deletion request is processed, the following data may be deleted:

  • DinoStream account information
  • Profile information
  • Authentication records associated with the account
  • Premium entitlement records
  • DinoReward-related account data
  • Stream preferences and settings
  • Connected account information stored by DinoStream

Data that may be retained

Some information may be retained for a limited period when required for:

  • Legal obligations
  • Fraud prevention
  • Security purposes
  • Financial or transaction records
Retained information will only be stored for as long as necessary under applicable laws and policies.

Processing time

Account deletion requests are generally processed within 30 days.

Contact

For account deletion requests or privacy-related questions, contact:
support@dinoapps.in
