DinoReward

The rewards/account system associated with DinoStream.

DinoReward is the rewards and account system associated with DinoStream. Within the App, users may see information such as:

  • Dino Points
  • Loyalty information
  • Premium status

Redeeming Dino Points

Dino Points can currently be redeemed toward Premium as follows:

  • 10 Dino Points → 7 days of Premium
  • 50 Dino Points → 50% discount on one month of Premium
Redeeming Dino Points is different from purchasing a Premium plan directly. See the Premium page for paid plan pricing and details.

For questions about DinoReward, visit the Support page.
