Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DinoStream ("the App", "we", "our") handles information when you use the DinoStream mobile application and related services such as DinoReward.

1. Information We Process

DinoStream may process the following categories of information:

  • Google account information: When you sign in with Google, we receive basic profile information (such as your name, email address, and profile picture) as provided by Google's authentication service, used to identify your account within DinoStream.
  • DinoReward account information: Information associated with your DinoReward account, such as your reward status and points balance, where applicable.
  • YouTube account information: If you explicitly choose to connect a YouTube account, we process information necessary to enable that connection and any related streaming features. This only occurs after you take an explicit action to connect your account.
  • Streaming-related information: Technical information related to your use of streaming features within the App (for example, stream session status).
  • Basic account/session information: Information needed to keep you signed in and to operate your account within the App, such as session identifiers.

We only describe here the categories of data that DinoStream, as a mobile streaming application, may reasonably process in connection with the features described on this website. We do not claim to collect data beyond what is necessary for these features.

2. How We Use Information

  • To authenticate you and operate your DinoStream account.
  • To provide DinoReward account features, where applicable.
  • To enable YouTube-connected features that you have explicitly turned on.
  • To operate and maintain screen/gameplay streaming functionality.
  • To provide customer support when you contact us.

3. Google Sign-In

DinoStream uses Google Sign-In to authenticate users. DinoStream does not ask for, and does not have access to, your Google account password. Your credentials are handled entirely by Google's own authentication systems.

4. Advertising (Free Users)

DinoStream may use third-party advertising services to display ads to users on the Free tier. Users who upgrade to Premium do not see ads. Details of the specific advertising provider(s) and the exact data such services process (for example, via an SDK such as Google AdMob) will be published here once that implementation is finalized. Until then, we do not claim any specific advertising data collection beyond what is generally necessary for an ad-supported free tier to function.

5. Third-Party Services

DinoStream integrates with Google and YouTube services. These are third-party services operated by Google, and your use of them is also subject to Google's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. We encourage you to review Google's privacy practices independently.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information is used to operate DinoStream's features as described above. We do not share data with third parties except where required to provide the connected services you have explicitly enabled (such as Google or YouTube), where required to operate third-party advertising for Free-tier users as described above, or where required by law.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information processed by DinoStream. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

We retain account-related information for as long as your DinoStream account is active, or as needed to provide you with the App's features.

9. Your Choices & Account Deletion

You may disconnect your YouTube account connection at any time within the App.

To request deletion of your DinoStream account and associated data, email us at ritikqe12@gmail.com with your account details and a request to delete your account. Deletion requests are currently handled manually by our team; we do not currently offer an automated in-app deletion tool.

10. Children's Privacy

DinoStream is not directed at children under the age required by applicable law to consent to data processing, and we do not knowingly collect information from such users.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
