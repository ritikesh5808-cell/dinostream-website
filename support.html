Common Support Categories

🔐 Google Sign-In problems

▶️ YouTube connection problems

📡 Streaming problems

Trouble starting, maintaining, or ending a screen/gameplay stream.

🦴 DinoReward problems

Questions or issues related to your DinoReward account or points.

⭐ Premium problems

Account Deletion

To request deletion of your DinoStream account and associated data, email us at ritikqe12@gmail.com with your account details and a request to delete your account. We will process your request manually.

When contacting support, please include your DinoStream account email and a brief description of the issue. Do not send passwords or verification codes by email.
